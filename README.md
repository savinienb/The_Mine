# The Mine

A 3D browser-based survival horror game featuring spatial occlusion acoustics, real-time candle smoke airflow navigation, and 62 real recorded CC0 horror & mine foley stems — playable on **desktop**, **mobile phones / tablets**, and **gamepads**.

## Play Online

Play directly in your browser (desktop or phone) via GitHub Pages:
**[https://savinienb.github.io/The_Mine/](https://savinienb.github.io/The_Mine/)**

---

### Controls

#### Mobile / Touchscreen (Portrait & Landscape)
- **Left Virtual Thumbstick**: Drag anywhere in the bottom-left zone to move smoothly; push to the outer ring (or hold **» RUN**) to sprint.
- **Right Free-Look Pad**: Drag anywhere on the right half of the screen to look around freely (full multi-touch while moving).
- **Hold `⚒ DIG`**: Mine soft/slate rock seams or strike timber supports (3 strikes collapse a bay); drag while holding to aim.
- **Hold `◎ LISTEN`**: Stand still, amplify footsteps/breathing in the dark, and drag to turn until the candle smoke curls back toward you (pointing toward the exit shaft).
- **`⛭ HIDE / LEAVE / WIND`**: Contextual button — tap near an ore cart to hide inside, or hold at the exit hoist to crank the lift.
- **Top Utility Bar**: **`🕯 FLAME`** (cover/uncover candle), **`🧨 TNT`** (plant charge), **`🗺 MAP`** (chalk map, tap map to close), **`? NOTES`**, and **`❚❚ PAUSE`**.

#### Keyboard & Mouse / Controller
- **Move / Sprint**: `WASD` / Left Stick (`Shift` / `L3` or `RB` to sprint)
- **Look**: Mouse / Right Stick
- **Dig / Strike Timber**: Hold `Left Click` / `RT`
- **Stand Still & Listen**: Hold `F` / `LT`
- **Hide in Cart / Wind Hoist**: `E` / `A`
- **Candle / TNT / Chalk Map**: `C` (`Y`), `R` (`X`), `M` (`View`)
