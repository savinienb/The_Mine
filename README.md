# The Mine

A 3D browser-based survival horror game set in the collapsed 1891 lower workings — featuring dynamic candle-smoke airflow navigation, smooth WebGL lighting & shadows, three distinct subterranean predators, an illustrated colliery survival carton, and 62 real recorded CC0 horror & mine foley stems. Playable on **desktop**, **mobile phones / tablets**, and **gamepads**.

## How to Survive (The Candle & The Dark)

1. **Smoke Compass (`Hold F` / `LT` / `◎ LISTEN`)**: Fresh air flows down from the surface Hoist Cage. Stand still, hold Listen, and turn slowly — when the pale candle smoke **curls straight back toward your chest**, the tunnel ahead is the shortest open route to the Hoist.
2. **Predator Alarm**: Watch your flame. When a creature draws close, the candle shrinks, gutters, and shivers before you see anything in the dark.
3. **Snuff to Vanish (`C` / `B` / `🕯 FLAME`)**: The pale **Ghost (Mourner)** spots a lit candle from **25 m** away and phases straight through solid stone walls toward it. Pinch out your flame to drop her sight to **5 m** and conserve wax.
4. **Ghost Wall-Phasing**: Listen for a cold stone-sigh and low rumble inside nearby rock — the Ghost makes directional wall sounds while passing through stone and **cannot grab you until her entire body steps clear of the wall**.
5. **Dig, Collapse & Escape**: Use your unbreakable pickaxe (`Hold LMB` / `RT` / `⚒`) to cut Ochre & Slate seams or smash timber supports **3 times** to cave in a gallery on pursuers. Save your **1× TNT** (`R` / `RB` / `🧨`) for dark Basalt walls, hide inside iron ore carts (`E` / `A` / `⛭`), and **hold `E` at the Hoist Cage** to crank the noisy winch to daylight.

## Controls

### Mobile / Touchscreen (Portrait & Landscape)
- **Left Virtual Thumbstick**: Drag anywhere in the bottom-left zone to move smoothly; push to the outer ring or tap **`» RUN`** to sprint.
- **`» RUN` (Toggle)**: Tap once to lock sprint **ON** (`RUN ON`) hands-free so your right thumb stays free to look around; tap again to turn sprint **OFF**.
- **Right Free-Look Pad**: Drag anywhere on the right half of the screen to look around freely while moving or running.
- **Hold `⚒ DIG`**: Mine Ochre/Slate rock seams or strike timber supports (3 hits collapse a bay).
- **Hold `◎ LISTEN`**: Stand still, amplify footsteps/breathing in the dark, and turn until candle smoke curls back toward you.
- **`⛭ HIDE / LEAVE / WIND`**: Tap near an iron ore cart to hide inside, or hold at the exit hoist to crank the lift.
- **Top Utility Bar**: **`🕯 FLAME`** (snuff/relight candle), **`🧨 TNT`** (plant charge), **`🗺 MAP`** (chalk map, tap map to close), **`? GUIDE`** (Illustrated Survival Carton), and **`❚❚ PAUSE`**.

### Keyboard & Mouse / Controller
- **Move / Sprint**: `WASD` / Left Stick (`Shift` / `LB` to sprint)
- **Look**: Mouse / Right Stick
- **Smoke Compass & Listen**: Hold `F` / `LT`
- **Snuff / Relight Candle**: `C` / `B`
- **Hide in Cart / Wind Hoist**: `E` / `A`
- **Dig Seam / Smash Timber (×3)**: Hold `Left Click` / `RT`
- **Plant 1× TNT**: `R` / `RB`
- **Chalk Map / Survival Guide / Pause**: `M` (`Y`), `H`, `Esc` (`Menu`)
